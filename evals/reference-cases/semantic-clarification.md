# Late ambiguity and stale-approval collision case

A red review finds that mixed-version compatibility admits two plausible intended behaviors that the approved baseline did not decide. The finding arrives while unrelated implementation work is also in progress. Repository evidence can prove the two behaviors and their compatibility effects, but cannot choose product intent.

A good first attempt classifies the finding rather than treating it automatically as a coding defect, records one stable ambiguity with competing interpretations and affected acceptance/scope IDs, stops only the affected work, returns the content-bound packet to awaiting approval, increments the requirement revision, and preserves approval history. It obtains the user's disposition and creates fresh digest-bound Requirement Ready and design approvals before affected implementation resumes. Unaffected work may continue when its inputs and evidence remain valid; reviewers and workers may surface interpretations but never resolve user-owned semantics.
