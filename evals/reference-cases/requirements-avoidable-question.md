# Avoidable clarification negative case

The request, current contract, and deterministic tests agree on one reversible local behavior change. Repository instructions identify the exact component and no security, compatibility, product, scope, dependency, or delivery alternative survives inspection.

A good first attempt records the evidence-backed execute-mode baseline, bounded acceptance and protected behavior, then proceeds without ceremonial clarification. It preserves normal verification and delivery-authority boundaries and states honest execution limitations, but does not manufacture ambiguity or ask naming, architecture, UI, or delivery questions that cannot affect the bounded outcome.
